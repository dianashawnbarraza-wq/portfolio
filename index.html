About

Over a decade ago, I opened the book Don’t Make Me Think. Ever since, I’ve been drawn to designing products that make complex systems feel obvious.

Throughout my career, I’ve turned complicated banking, healthcare, and real estate workflows into experiences people can understand and use. I bring together user research, behavioral data, and product strategy to uncover the right problems and move quickly toward thoughtful solutions.

I work closely with cross-functional teams to bring clarity to ambiguous problems, align around a direction, and move from strategy to launch. I’m equally comfortable shaping what’s next as I am working in code alongside engineering to bring zero-to-one products, new features, and improvements to life. At Square, I built my AI builder chops, and at Brain Co. I continue to build on those skills to deploy first-class enterprise software to customers.
